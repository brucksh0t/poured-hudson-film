How a pour works

  1. 1 · BOOK

    Reserve a seat for everyone in your party; each person makes their own fragrance (no shared experiences). Groups of 5 or more should call before booking online.

  2. 2 · BLEND

    Staff help you build a scent combination from 40+ fragrances, made with perfume-grade fragrance and essential oils. Wine, beer, and cider are available at the bar. No outside food or drink.

  3. 3 · POUR

    A booking includes an 8 oz candle in amber or black glass. At the bar you can upgrade to larger tinted glass, ceramic, iron, marble, or stone vessels, or to a cologne, perfume, or reed diffuser. Upgrade prices vary: ask at the bar.

  4. 4 · PICK UP

    Candles cure for about 120 minutes. If you start within 3 hours of closing, pick up the next day or pay to have it shipped.
