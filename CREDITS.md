Each film carries an on-screen label: "Illustrative · AI-generated from the shop's photos". Booking facts sit beside the footage (desktop) or below it (phones), on a dark scrim, never over the product.

Frames are WebP, cut after a 2× upscale of the 720p clips: `x/` 2560×1440 for large or high-DPR desktop screens, `d/` 1920×1080, and `m/` 1080×1440 crops for phones (WebP q72–78; desktop tiers q76–85). Browsers with AVIF support get AVIF copies of the same frames instead: each is encoded at the lowest quality whose SSIM against the WebP frame is at least 0.94–0.975 (0.94 for the photo pan on phones), with a quality floor of 24 on phones and 30 on desktop. The phone frames are true 1080×1440 crops of the 2× frames (no resize). Hero and wick were upscaled with a commercial video upscaler; the pour clip, the tray crop and the hero side pan with an open-source 2× upscaler (Real-ESRGAN x2plus model) (the 1080×1440 phone crop is therefore a 2× upscale of a 540×720 region of the 720p clip). The page picks a tier from the canvas CSS size × devicePixelRatio (capped at 2).

Loading: nothing from `frames/` is requested until the first scroll. Each tier has a light preview ladder (`mq/` 360×480 and `dq/` 480×270, WebP q40, every 3rd frame, plus a `mt/`/`dt/` micro ladder at q30, every 6th frame, used only on quick flicks) that is fetched first, closest to the reader first; the sharp frame replaces a preview as soon as it lands. Films load only when they are within 2.5 screens. The canvas always draws the nearest frame on hand, and the poster sits under it. The hero poster is its first frame. With Save-Data on or a 2G connection, the page shows the stills instead of the films. When reduced motion is on, or JS is off, the page shows the stills and all of the content without scroll-scrubbing.

Fonts are self-hosted (Cormorant Garamond and Karla, SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts) with font-display: swap and metric-matched local fallbacks (size-adjust and ascent/descent overrides), so text and the hero CTA paint at once.
